# Harichand Steel - Company Information & Technical Profile

> Harichand Steel is a premier architectural metal fabrication company headquartered in New Delhi, India. Established in 2004, the firm specializes in engineering aluminium profile gates, stainless steel main gates, high-security steel doors, and motorized entrance automation systems.

## Corporate History & Background

Founded in 2004, Harichand Steel began with a vision to deliver uncompromised fabrication quality for residential and commercial gate systems. Over the course of more than two decades, the company has expanded its manufacturing floor in Uttam Nagar, New Delhi, serving over 10,000+ residential owners, architects, and real estate developers across Delhi NCR.

---

## Manufacturing Capabilities & Tooling

Harichand Steel operates an integrated fabrication facility equipped with modern machinery:

1. **CNC Laser Cutting:** High-power fiber laser machines capable of cutting intricate geometric, floral, and modern architectural patterns into heavy-gauge carbon steel and SS304 plates.
2. **Precision Extrusion Assembly:** Automated profiling and assembly for lightweight 6063-T6 architectural aluminium extrusions.
3. **Advanced TIG/MIG Welding:** Argon-shielded TIG welding for seamless, polished joints without slag or discoloration.
4. **Electrostatic Powder Coating Booth:** High-temperature curing oven producing UV-resistant matte black, antique bronze, and European wood-grain textures.
5. **Automation & Smart Testing Bench:** Quality testing for Italian gate motors (Sommer, Nice, BFT) and optical biometric locks.

---

## Material Standards & Engineering Integrity

- **Stainless Steel:** Strictly authentic SS304 grade austenitic stainless steel (18% Chromium, 8% Nickel) providing total resistance to rust and oxidation.
- **Aluminium Profiles:** High-tensile 6063-T6 architectural alloy offering high rigidity and 60% weight reduction compared to solid iron.
- **Forged Wrought Iron:** Heavy-gauge carbon steel hot-forged with anti-corrosive zinc phosphate primers.
- **Hardware:** Sealed stainless-steel ball bearing hinges capable of handling gates up to 1,500 kg with minimal friction.

---

## Quality Assurance & Turnkey Services

Every gate manufactured undergoes a 5-point quality checklist:
1. Structural squareness and weld penetration verification.
2. Surface smoothness and powder-coating adhesion test.
3. Hinge load and deflection analysis.
4. Lock alignment and keyless biometric sensor test.
5. Motor torque and safety auto-reverse testing.
