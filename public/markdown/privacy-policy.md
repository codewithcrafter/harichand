# Privacy Policy - Harichand Steel

> Harichand Steel is committed to safeguarding customer privacy. This policy outlines how we collect, handle, and protect client information through our website and quotation inquiries.

## 2. Inquiries & Contact Forms
When visitors submit contact forms or inquiry requests on the website, we collect the data entered in the form (name, phone number, email address, gate dimensions, and project location) solely to calculate fabrication quotes and arrange doorstep site measurements.

## 3. Media
If you upload images or CAD drawings of your gate opening, we use those files strictly for technical engineering evaluation. We do not sell or distribute client media.

## 4. Cookies
Our website uses essential performance and navigation cookies to ensure proper display across mobile and desktop devices. If you interact with our administrative or inquiry panels, session cookies may be stored temporarily.

## 5. Embedded Content
Articles or guides on this site may include embedded content (e.g. videos, maps). Embedded content from third-party sites behaves in the exact same manner as if the visitor visited the third-party website.

## 6. How Long We Retain Your Data
Inquiry information is retained in our secure local customer management system to maintain communication during your project fabrication and future warranty service.

## 7. Your Data Rights
You may request an exported file of the personal data we hold about you or request that we erase any personal data we hold about you.
