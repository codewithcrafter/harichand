## Why Choose Harichand Steel

Harichand Steel engineers precision architectural gates built with certified SS304 alloys, Italian automation compatibility, and generational durability.

### 1. Superior Craftsmanship
Precision-engineered with master detailing, robotic welding, and seamless joinery. Every gate is built to withstand high cycle frequencies and harsh Delhi weather without structural fatigue.

### 2. SS304 Premium Alloys
100% rust-free chromium-nickel composition (18% chromium, 8% nickel) delivering maximum corrosion resistance and structural endurance.

### 3. Aluminium Profile Gates
Concealed heavy-gauge extruded aluminium framing with rose-gold accents, fluted glass infills, and lightweight operation that reduces hinge strain by 60%.

### 4. Italian Motorized Automation
Compatible with premium Italian gate automation operators (Sommer, Nice, BFT) supporting smartphone Wi-Fi control, obstacle-detection radar sensors, and battery backup.

### 5. Digital Biometric Locks
Ultra-fast 0.3s optical fingerprint recognition, RFID chip entry, encrypted passcode entry, and real-time Tuya mobile app synchronization.
