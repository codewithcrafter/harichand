## Types of Modern Steel Gate Designs

Steel gates have evolved far beyond basic metal bars. At Harichand Steel, our fabrication workshop produces multiple distinctive gate design styles tailored to varying architectural languages:

### 1. Modern Steel Gates
Minimalist horizontal slat gates, integrated flush sheets, and contrasting metallic handles that match contemporary villa architecture. These gates offer clean sightlines, privacy without feeling claustrophobic, and smooth manual or motorized operation.

### 2. Laser Cut CNC Steel Gates
Intricate geometric, floral, or abstract patterns cut into heavy gauge steel plates with computerized precision. CNC plasma and fiber laser cutting enables exact artistic customization, allowing homeowners to incorporate personalized motifs, family crests, or modern geometric lattices.

### 3. Stainless Steel (SS304) Main Gates
Crafted using certified austenitic SS304 stainless steel containing 18% chromium and 8% nickel. Provides complete resistance against rainwater, high humidity, and smog, maintaining a brilliant polished or brushed satin sheen for decades.

### 4. Sliding & Swing Motorized Gates
Designed specifically to interface with Italian automation motors (Sommer, Nice, BFT). Features reinforced lower guide rails, heavy-duty nylon rollers, and anti-jump brackets for silent, dependable motorized gliding.

## Best Steel Gate Designs for Homes

When choosing the right gate, consider the following parameters:
- **Privacy Requirements:** Solid infill panels or closely spaced horizontal louvers prevent street-side visibility.
- **Wind Resistance:** Louvered or CNC perforated designs allow airflow, reducing lateral wind pressure on tall gates.
- **Weight & Bearing Longevity:** Combining steel frameworks with aluminium or composite inserts reduces load on foundation posts and hinges.

## Material Comparison: SS304 vs. SS202 vs. Mild Steel

| Material | Rust Resistance | Lifespan | Maintenance | Recommended Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SS304 Stainless Steel** | Excellent (Immune to rain & rust) | 25+ Years | Very Low (Occasional wipe) | Main entrance gates, outdoor railings |
| **SS202 Stainless Steel** | Moderate (May spot-rust outdoors) | 8-12 Years | Moderate | Covered indoor areas, interior stairs |
| **Mild Steel (MS)** | Low (Requires powder coat/paint) | 10-15 Years | Regular Repainting | Budget gates, structural frames |
| **Architectural Aluminium** | Total (Zero rust formation) | 30+ Years | Ultra Low | Modern profile gates, luxury villas |
