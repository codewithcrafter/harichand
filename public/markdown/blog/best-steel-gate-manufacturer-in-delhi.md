## Stainless Steel Gates for Homes: Advantages

Stainless steel main gates remain the #1 choice for modern Delhi homeowners due to several critical factors:
- **Total Weather Resistance:** Delhi experiences scorching 45°C+ summer heat followed by heavy monsoon humidity. SS304 does not rust, flake, warp, or discolor.
- **Low Maintenance:** Does not require periodic repainting. An occasional soap-and-water wipe restores its original luster.
- **Architectural Appeal:** Perfectly complements contemporary facade materials including Italian marble, HPL sheets, ACP cladding, and exterior texture paint.
