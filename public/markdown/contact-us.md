## Doorstep Site Inspection & Quotation Process

1. **Inquiry Submission:** Contact us via phone, WhatsApp, or email with your approximate gate dimensions and preferred design.
2. **Doorstep Site Visit:** Our technical engineer visits your property anywhere in Delhi NCR to measure ground levels, hinge pillar alignment, and clearance.
3. **Design Approval & Estimate:** We provide detailed material specifications, CAD drawings, and fixed factory pricing.
4. **Precision Fabrication:** Your gate is custom-built at our Uttam Nagar manufacturing facility.
5. **Turnkey Installation:** Our team handles alignment, motorization testing, and handover.
