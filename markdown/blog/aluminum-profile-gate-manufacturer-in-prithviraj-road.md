## Bespoke Architectural Gateways for Heritage & Diplomatic Residences

Prithviraj Road and the surrounding Lutyens' Delhi sector demand entrance gates that meet the highest standards of architectural dignity, privacy, and unobtrusive security. Harichand Steel fabricates custom high-end gate systems tailored for:

### 1. Acoustic Dampening & Privacy Infill
Bespoke profile designs incorporating sound-deadening architectural cores and precision louver angles that eliminate street visibility while maintaining subtle airflow.

### 2. Concealed Automation & Advanced Security
Equipped with concealed in-ground or pillar-mounted Italian motorized operators, ensuring zero visible machinery. Fully compatible with perimeter radar sensors, license plate recognition cameras, and encrypted biometric authentication.

### 3. Noble Metallic & Wood Finishes
Finished with architectural fluoropolymer coatings, brushed rose gold, antique bronze, and European natural grain powder textures engineered to retain color vibrancy for decades under intense UV exposure.
