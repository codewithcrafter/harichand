## Why Choose Aluminium Profile Gates?

Traditional iron gates are prone to rust and require frequent repainting, while heavy solid gates place immense stress on automated motors and hinges. Aluminium profile gates have rapidly become the preferred choice for Janakpuri homeowners because:

### 1. Zero Rust & Corrosion
Aluminium naturally forms a protective oxide layer, making it completely impervious to rainwater, moisture, and sunlight. Unlike iron or mild steel, it never rusts, even after decades of exposure.

### 2. Lightweight Operation
Aluminium profile gates are 60% lighter than solid iron or heavy steel gates. This significant weight reduction:
- Drastically reduces wear and tear on hinge bearings.
- Prevents post sagging and misalignments.
- Allows smaller, faster, and more affordable automation motors to operate effortlessly.

### 3. Modern Luxury Aesthetics
Architectural aluminium profiles can be powder-coated in rich European wood-grain textures, matte black, graphite grey, or champagne gold. Combined with frosted glass or rose-gold handles, they transform any residence into a modern luxury statement.
