# About Harichand Steel

> Harichand Steel has built a strong reputation as a trusted manufacturer of aluminium profile gates and premium steel gate solutions in Delhi NCR since 2004.

- **Original Page URL:** [https://harichandsteel.com/about-us/](https://harichandsteel.com/about-us/)

- **Founding Location:** Uttam Nagar, New Delhi

## Experts Dedicated to Your Success

We are trusted steel gate fabricators based in Delhi, specializing in premium steel gates, aluminum profile gates, and rolling shutters. Established in 2004, Harichand Steel has spent more than two decades perfecting metal fabrication, delivering architectural excellence for luxury homes, residential estates, and commercial buildings.

Our commitment to quality, modern design, and precision craftsmanship has made us a preferred choice for durable and stylish gate manufacturing across Delhi and Delhi NCR.

---

## Core Pillars of Our Manufacturing

### 1. Durability
Engineered to resist harsh weather, monsoons, intense summer heat, and heavy daily usage without sagging, fading, or rattling.

### 2. Structural Strength
Fabricated using heavy-gauge SS304 stainless steel, reinforced architectural aluminium profiles, and solid mild steel structural frameworks.

### 3. Customer Trust
Thousands of satisfied homeowners, architects, and builders rely on our prompt delivery, precise doorstep measurements, and transparent factory pricing.

### 4. Strong Engineering Foundation
Equipped with state-of-the-art TIG/MIG welding machines, computer-controlled CNC profiling, automated powder-coating lines, and seamless gate automation hardware.

## Comprehensive Metal Fabrication Services

- **Steel Gates:** Certified SS304 main gates featuring modern laser-cut CNC designs and heavy-duty ball bearing hinges.
- **Aluminium Profile Gates:** Lightweight, rust-free profile gates with wood-grain or rose-gold accents.
- **Steel Doors:** High-security exterior steel doors with multi-point mortise lock systems.
- **Steel Grills:** Window security grills, boundary wall railings, and decorative patterns.
- **Steel Staircases & Railings:** Indoor and outdoor spiral or straight steel stairs with architectural glass and SS railings.
- **Rolling Shutters:** Motorized and manual security shutters for commercial and industrial storefronts.
