## Product Categories Overview

Harichand Steel categorizes its architectural metal fabrication into five dedicated divisions:

1. **[Aluminum Profile Gates](https://harichandsteel.com/product-category/aluminum-profile-gate/)** ([Markdown Guide](categories/aluminum-profile-gate.md))
   - Lightweight, zero-rust architectural aluminium extrusions with wood-grain or metallic finishes.
2. **[Steel Main Gates](https://harichandsteel.com/product-category/steel-main-gate/)** ([Markdown Guide](categories/steel-main-gate.md))
   - Certified SS304 stainless steel and CNC laser-cut heavy gauge entry gates.
3. **[Iron Main Gates](https://harichandsteel.com/product-category/iron-main-gate/)** ([Markdown Guide](categories/iron-main-gate.md))
   - Heavy-duty forged wrought iron and cast iron gates with classical security spikes and scrolls.
4. **[Steel Doors](https://harichandsteel.com/product-category/steel-door/)** ([Markdown Guide](categories/steel-door.md))
   - High-security residential entrance steel doors with multi-point locking systems.
5. **[Powder Coated Gates](https://harichandsteel.com/product-category/powder-coated-gates/)** ([Markdown Guide](categories/powder-coated-gates.md))
   - Weatherproof electrostatic powder-coated gates in matte black, antique bronze, and wood-grain.

## Custom Sizing & Manufacturing Capabilities

Every gate is custom-built to match client specifications:
- **Measurements:** Precise doorstep site measurement across Delhi & Delhi NCR.
- **Hardware:** SS304 heavy-duty ball bearing hinges, concealed flush bolts, and anti-sag cantilever designs.
- **Smart Access:** Pre-configured for biometric access, RFID readers, and motorized remote gate openers (Sommer, Nice, BFT).
